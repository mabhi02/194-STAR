You are working with a database that contains information about customers, orders, books, and book orders. The database has four tables:

  • customers: Contains customer information including ID, first name, last name, email, city, state, country, and registration date
  • orders: Contains order information including order ID, customer ID, order date, total amount, and status
  • books: Contains book information including ID, title, price, publication year, and author ID
  • book_orders: Contains book order information including ID, book ID, quantity, and order date

Use the SQL Builder below to answer the following questions:

Question 1 (EASY): Retrieve the first name, last name, and country of all customers.

Question 2 (EASY): Find the first name, last name, and city of customers who live in the USA.

Question 3 (EASY): Find the title, price, and publication year for all books with a price greater than 50.

Question 4 (MEDIUM): Group customers by country and count how many are from each country.

Question 5 (MEDIUM): Find the title, order date, and quantity for each book order.

Question 6 (MEDIUM): Find the title, price, and publication year for all books with a price between 10 and 20 exclusive.

Question 7 (HARD): Group together books by publication year, and report the publication year, average price, and number of books for each year. Order the results by publication year in ascending order.

Question 8 (HARD): For each customer, show their full name and total amount spent across all orders.

Question 9 (HARD): Find customers who have spent more than $500 in total. Show full name and total spent, ordered by total spent descending.

The correct answers are:

-- Question 1 SELECT first_name, last_name, country FROM customers; -- Question 2 SELECT first_name, last_name, city FROM customers WHERE country = 'USA'; -- Question 3 SELECT title, price, publication_year FROM books WHERE price > 50; -- Question 4 SELECT country, COUNT(*) AS customer_count FROM customers GROUP BY country; -- Question 5 SELECT b.title, bo.order_date, bo.quantity FROM book_orders bo JOIN books b ON bo.book_id = b.id; -- Question 6 SELECT title, price, publication_year FROM books WHERE price > 10 AND price < 20; -- Question 7 SELECT publication_year, AVG(price) AS average_price, COUNT(*) AS number_books FROM books GROUP BY publication_year ORDER BY publication_year ASC; -- Question 8 SELECT c.first_name, c.last_name, SUM(o.total_amount) AS total_spent FROM customers c JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.first_name, c.last_name; -- Question 9 SELECT c.first_name, c.last_name, SUM(o.total_amount) AS total_spent FROM customers c JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.first_name, c.last_name HAVING total_spent > 500 ORDER BY total_spent DESC;
